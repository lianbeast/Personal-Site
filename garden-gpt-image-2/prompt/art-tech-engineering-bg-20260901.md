# Artistic Technology Engineering Background

## Prompt

A seamless, tileable background texture for a dark sci-fi website. Deep black (#0A0A0A) base. Abstract, atmospheric engineering/technology motif — think: faint blueprint grid lines, subtle circuit trace patterns, ghosted geometric wireframes (triangles, hexagons, coordinate axes), and soft radial light leaks in warm gold (#D4AF37) and cool cyan (#00B4D8). The patterns should be semi-transparent, ethereal, like a holographic overlay on dark glass. No text, no logos, no people, no readable UI. Style: cyberpunk meets architectural drafting. Noise grain texture overlay at 5% opacity for tactile quality. Aspect ratio 16:9, suitable for full-bleed web background at 1920×1080 or larger.

## Parameters

- **Size**: 1536×1024 (16:9 landscape)
- **Quality**: high
- **Style**: abstract, atmospheric, technical

## Usage

This is a website background — keep all elements semi-transparent and subtle. The black base should dominate (~85% of the image). Gold and cyan accents should be rare, delicate, and never compete with foreground text.

## Where to paste

Use with GPT Image 2, DALL·E 3, Midjourney, or any image gen tool with this prompt.
